# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-18

- Add product registration and configurable reorder levels.
- Record purchases and sales in a SQLite stock ledger.
- Reject invalid prices, quantities, and sales above available stock.
- Show stock balances, low-stock items, and movement history.
- Export the movement ledger as CSV.
- Include a temporary-data demo, tests, and Linux/Windows checks.
